Food and Its Importance Food is one of the basic needs of human beings. It gives us energy to work, play and stay healthy. There are different types of food such as fruits, vegetables, grains, milk, eggs and meat. Healthy food contains important nutrients like vitamins, proteins, carbohydrates and minerals. Eating fresh and nutritious food helps us grow stronger and protects us from diseases. We should eat a balanced diet, drink plenty of water and avoid eating too much junk food. Healthy eating habits help us live a happy and healthy life.
